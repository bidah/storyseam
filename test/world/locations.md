# Locations

## theo-living-room
The whole film happens here. Late afternoon, low sun through a west window throwing a
long amber rectangle across the floor. A worn teal couch with removable cushions. A rug
buried under board games, half of them unopened. A bookshelf. Dust in the light.
Lived-in, warm, and — for about a minute — completely out of ideas.

## fort-interior
The same room, from inside the blanket fort, after the turn. Couch cushions form the
walls. The star-blanket is the ceiling, and its pinprick stars are *actually* stars now
— the fabric has gone to real depth, real black, real drifting nebula. A gap between two
cushions reads as a viewport. Warm amber light from the room leaks in at the floor edge,
so we never lose the fact that this is still a living room.

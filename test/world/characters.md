# Characters

## Theo
- **slug:** `theo`
- **age:** 8
- **role:** Host. It is his living room, his blanket, and his idea.
- **want:** For this afternoon to be good. He invited Milo; the flatness is his fault.
- **look:** Small for eight. Warm brown skin, tight black curls cropped close. Faded
  mustard t-shirt with a washed-out rocket on it, dark green shorts, mismatched socks,
  no shoes. Slightly too-big front teeth.
- **voice DNA:** Fast, up at the end of sentences, thinks out loud. Restarts himself
  mid-thought. When the idea lands, he goes *quiet* — that is his tell, and it is the
  turn of the whole film.

## Milo
- **slug:** `milo`
- **age:** 8
- **role:** The guest. Arrives full of expectation, has nowhere to put it.
- **want:** To be invited back.
- **look:** Taller, pale, freckled, straight sandy hair falling in his eyes. Blue
  hoodie with the sleeves pulled over his hands, jeans, red sneakers he keeps on
  indoors. Carries a small grey backpack he never opens.
- **voice DNA:** Slower. States things flatly and a little too honestly, then looks
  worried he said the wrong thing. Almost never asks questions.

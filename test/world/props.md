# Props

## star-blanket
The hinge of the entire film, so it must be identical in every block it appears in.
A well-worn navy fleece blanket printed with small pale-yellow five-pointed stars,
irregularly spaced. Frayed satin binding along one edge, one corner visibly repaired.
Folded over the back of the teal couch in blocks 1 and 2; seen, taken and thrown in
block 3; the ceiling of the world in block 4.

---
description: Prompt presets — the camera every shot is made with, the audio track, and shared presets by URL
---
Load the `camera` skill and follow it. Open `npx storyseam preset page --open`, recommend
two or three camera looks, say the audio setting (silent by default), offer (never render
unasked) a sample still per look, and install picks with `npx storyseam preset add
<section>/<id>` or `add <url>`.
